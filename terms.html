Terms & Conditions

Draft for the pilot website · Review before launch

These draft terms describe the current HelpKaro23 website, an independent community project. By using the website or submitting a request, you agree to use it lawfully and honestly. This is not legal advice and the terms should be reviewed for your actual operating model before launch.

1. What HelpKaro23 does

HelpKaro23 provides a place to submit and potentially publish summaries of requests for small financial support. Requests may be reviewed manually. Submission does not guarantee approval, publication, donors, or any amount of funding.

2. Organisation status

HelpKaro23 is not represented as a registered charity or NGO. The website does not promise tax deductions or charitable receipts. A listing is not a guarantee that a person's circumstances or claims have been independently proven.

3. Accuracy and misuse

Applicants must provide accurate information, have permission to submit another person's information, and notify the site administrator of material changes. Fraudulent, misleading, unlawful, abusive, duplicate, or unsafe requests may be rejected or removed.

4. Publication and consent

A request will not be publicly listed unless the applicant has affirmatively consented to publication of the specified summary and public fields. Consent to publish a story does not automatically grant permission to publish a photograph, identity document, phone number, email address, bank information, or other sensitive information. Obtain separate permission for photographs and identifying details.

5. Payments and donations

Current pilot status: this website does not accept, hold, route, or distribute donations. Do not send money to the website owner based on this site. Any future payment feature should be introduced only after the payment flow, provider approval, applicable legal and tax requirements, records, refunds and complaints process have been reviewed and these terms updated.

6. No guarantee or endorsement

Review is not a guarantee of the truth of every statement, identity of every person, or outcome of a request. Users and donors should exercise their own judgement and report suspicious activity. Listings may be removed to protect users or correct information.

7. Privacy notice

Information submitted through an external Google Form is processed through Google services and is accessible to the form owner and any authorised collaborators. Submit only information needed to review your request. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full card details, or unnecessary identity documents. Public pages should contain only fields the applicant explicitly approved. The administrator should restrict access to responses, establish a retention/deletion schedule, and provide a contact route for correction or deletion requests before launch.

8. Minors

People under 18 should use a parent/guardian-assisted review process. Do not publish identifying or sensitive details about children.

9. Availability and changes

The website is provided on an as-available basis. Features, request listings and these terms may change. Published requests may be paused or removed for safety or accuracy concerns.

10. Contact

For questions, corrections, removal requests or safety concerns, contact the HelpKaro23 administrator using the official contact email once configured. Do not send private identity documents unless specifically requested through a secure, restricted process.

Before public launch: add a working contact email, confirm the privacy and retention process, and obtain appropriate advice before receiving or redistributing donations.
